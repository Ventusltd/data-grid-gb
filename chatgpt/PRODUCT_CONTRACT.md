# Consumer contract

Mobile is the sales surface; it must answer immediately.

The immediate answer may contain only identity and declared connection facts
already present in a verified project record. ETYS enrichment may arrive
asynchronously and must retain source edition, scenario, node identifier,
voltage, exact metric name, and unit.

Appendix B is an authoritative topology and equipment-parameter feed. It is
not, by itself, a runnable or solved whole-GB power-flow case. A consumer must
not label reconstructed topology, impedance, or seasonal ratings as computed
power flow.

Appendix D publishes multiple fault-current measures. A consumer must not
collapse them into a single generic “fault level.”

Coordinates require an independently identified geospatial source and an
explicit mapping state. Unmatched and ambiguous identities go to review and
never reach a public map as verified connections.
